About · 关于我们

A Hangzhou export house for underwear and loungewear

Hansburg (汉宝) is the export name of 杭州汉宝贸易有限公司. We supply European retail partners with underwear and loungewear, and with the hang tags and packaging that travel with the goods.

The company is based in Hangzhou. Shipments are arranged FOB Shanghai. For a program conversation, write to Carrie.

Why Hansburg · 我们的优势

What we keep in view on every program

  1. 01

    EU compliance readiness

    Programs prepared for European retail requirements: the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR), heavy-metals testing, and EAN barcodes and care labels.

  2. 02

    Reliable OTIF

    On time, in full. Shipments planned so orders leave complete and on schedule for retail receiving.

  3. 03

    FOB Shanghai logistics

    A logistics network on FOB Shanghai terms, arranged from our base in Hangzhou.
