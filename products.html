Products

A catalog for wholesale programs

Underwear, seamless basics, loungewear, and the packaging that travels with them. Written for buyers in Europe and the United States.

Order size follows the style, the size run, and the packing. Tell Carrie the market and the assortment you have in mind, and she will confirm what a program can carry. A single minimum is not listed on this site.

The flagship fiber is bamboo-derived viscose, a regenerated cellulose in the rayon family. Other programs are described by construction until the style spec names the fiber. US labels for the flagship should use that viscose / rayon description. OEKO-TEX, REACH, GPSR, and PPWR can be part of a program brief. Certificate numbers are not shown here.
