What stays in view

Four parts of every program

  1. 01

    FOB Shanghai

    Shipments are quoted FOB Shanghai. The office that runs the program is in Hangzhou. You arrange freight from the Shanghai handover. Hansburg keeps the export side coherent up to that point.

  2. 02

    OTIF

    On time, in full. Orders are planned so a shipment can leave complete and on the date that was agreed, for a warehouse that receives against a purchase order.

  3. 03

    EU packaging readiness

    Packing and trim are prepared with European retail packaging rules in mind, including the Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR). Readiness means the program can be specified for those rules.

  4. 04

    Hang tags, care labels, EAN

    Retail trim can travel with the goods: hang tags, care labels, and EAN barcodes, so a carton is closer to shop-floor ready when it is received.

Process

Sampling, then production, then shipment

Timing is confirmed when the sample and the size run are agreed. A published house minimum is not part of this site.

  1. 01

    Sampling

    Handfeel, size, color, and trim are reviewed on samples before production is confirmed. The enquiry starts here: category, market, and season.

  2. 02

    Production

    The agreed spec is put into work. Quantities follow the size run in the brief. Orders are planned to leave complete.

  3. 03

    Shipment

    Goods are handed over FOB Shanghai, planned against the date on the order.
