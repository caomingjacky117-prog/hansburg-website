杭州汉宝贸易有限公司

Hansburg is the export name. The company is based in Hangzhou.

A Hangzhou export house for underwear and loungewear

Hansburg (汉宝) supplies wholesale buyers with underwear, seamless basics, and loungewear, and with the hang tags and packaging that travel with the goods.

The buyers are in Europe and the United States: retail partners, importers, distributors, pharmacy and organic retail, and Amazon private-label teams. Programs ship FOB Shanghai. Carrie handles the conversation directly.
